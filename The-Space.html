01The room

Light from the street, energy from the room.

Tall windows let the afternoon light in. Warm walls, quiet floors, and the chairs placed where the light lands.

Good music, real conversation, dryers going — the kind of room that lifts your mood before anyone touches your hair.

02The ritual

Incense first. Then the drink.

Every visit starts the same way: the incense is lit and your drink is poured for the time you booked — chosen when you book, ready when you arrive. Only then do we pick up a comb.

The incense is the one constant. Everything after it is yours to choose.

House notes — the few things that keep the room the way it is

01

Arrive five minutes early.

Your drink is poured for the time you booked. Late by more than fifteen and we may need to shorten the service so the next guest starts on time — message us on WhatsApp if you're running behind.

02

Quiet chairs are a thing.

Say "quiet, please" when you book and nobody will make small talk. Say "happy to chat" and we will.

03

IQOS only.

No cigarettes in the studio. Bring your IQOS — or ask, we keep one for guests.

04

Hijab-friendly, properly.

Tell us when you book and we make sure no man is in the studio for the length of your appointment — staff or guest. The whole room is yours.

05

Photos only with a yes.

We post our work. If you'd rather not appear, tick "no photos of me" at booking and the camera stays in the drawer.

06

Tell us what your skin knows.

Allergies, sensitivities, pregnancy, a recent colour or keratin — it changes what we can safely do. There's a place for all of it when you book.
