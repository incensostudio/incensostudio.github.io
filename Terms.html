01Appointments

Bookings are made online, by WhatsApp or in person, and are confirmed when you receive your booking number by SMS and WhatsApp. A booking holds a specific chair with specific hands for the time shown.

You can change or cancel a booking any time from your account or on WhatsApp. Cancelling with less than 12 hours' notice, or not showing up, may mean we ask for a deposit on your next booking.

We hold your chair for 15 minutes past the booked time. After that we may need to shorten the service so the next guest starts on time.

02Payments

You can pay at the studio (cash or card), or ahead by card, Whish Money or OMT Pay. Prices are in US dollars and are the prices shown at the time of booking.

If you paid ahead and later change your booking, the payment stays as it is: any extra is settled at the studio or paid online; any difference in your favour is refunded the same way you paid, within a few business days. Cancelled prepaid bookings are refunded in full the same way.

Members' discounts (Insider 10%, Loyal 20%) apply automatically to services and shelf purchases while your level is active. Levels are based on your spend in the last 12 months.

03The shelf

Products are sold as described, for studio pickup or courier delivery across Lebanon. Delivery terms are on the Shipping & Pickup page.

If something arrives damaged or wrong, message us within 7 days and we will replace or refund it. Opened cosmetics cannot be returned for change of mind — hygiene first.

If an item turns out to be out of stock after you order, we send the rest, refund the difference, and tell you. We never substitute without asking.

04Gift vouchers

Vouchers are valid for 12 months from purchase, on any service or shelf purchase, and can be used across several visits until the balance is spent. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash.

A voucher is tied to its code — treat it like cash. Lost codes can be recovered from the buyer's account.

05In the studio

The studio is IQOS-only — no cigarettes. Please tell us about allergies, sensitivities, pregnancy or recent chemical treatments when you book; it changes what we can safely do.

We photograph our work. If you would rather not appear, say so at booking or on the day and we won't.

We may decline or end a service if a guest is unwell, under the influence, or unkind to our team. You'd expect nothing less for yourself.
